# Arthur's Arena: Legends

Written 2026-11-05 for Arthur.

This is the big version of Arthur's Arena. It has a **Story** with nine chapters, an **Arcade** mode that never ends, a Smash-style **BRAWL** mode, four difficulty levels, three fighters to pick from, a charge-up **fireball**, spiky robots and spiky bosses, ninjas, falling weapons, combos, and a SUPER move.

The playable file is `projects/arthurs-arena-legends.sb3`. The boss game from before (`projects/arthurs-arena-bosses.sb3`, see `BOSSES.md`) is still there.

## Load it

1. Open the [Scratch editor](https://scratch.mit.edu/projects/editor/).
2. Choose **File**, then **Load from your computer**.
3. Pick `projects/arthurs-arena-legends.sb3`.
4. Click the green flag.

On the tablet, the file has to be on the Scratch website first. Sign in, load the file, and save it. Then open that project's link in the tablet browser.

## Power levels

Every fight you win gives you **XP**: +1 for a chapter or a wave, +2 for a boss or a BRAWL win. With enough XP your **POWER LEVEL** goes up (you'll see **POWER UP!**). The game remembers it.

| Power level | XP needed | Aura | New POWER move |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | start | dim red | (none yet) |
| 2 | 3 | orange | **FLAME DASH**: burn straight through the enemy |
| 3 | 7 | gold | **THUNDER STRIKE**: a bolt of lightning from the sky |
| 4 | 12 | blue, with sparks | **BLUE STORM**: three lightning bolts |
| 5 | 18 | purple-white, with lots of sparks | **ULTRA STORM**: five bolts, the last one huge |

- **Powering up (Dragon Ball style):** before every fight your fighter crouches, clenches their fists and yells **HAAAAA!**. The aura surges, the ground shakes and rocks rip up out of the floor. **The higher your power level, the bigger the show:** more rocks from further away, the ground shakes longer, and from level 2 the **ground cracks open** under your feet, a **shockwave** ring blasts out, and **giant boulders** tear loose and float up (at level 5 most of them are boulders). From **power level 3** they **transform**: a white flash, **SUPER ARTHUR!** (or Nova or Rocko), and **golden spiky hair** for the whole fight. At **level 5** it's **ULTRA**, with **silver hair**. Levelling up after a win plays the transformation too, a quick flare of power goes off when your SUPER bar fills, and every POWER move starts with a short power-up.
- **Your aura** shows your level. Each level is brighter, bigger and flickers faster. When your SUPER is full, or during a SUPER or POWER move, it **blazes** at full brightness.
- **Every level** gives you +10 health and +1 damage on every hit.
- **Enemies level up with you:** more health, harder hits, faster attacks. From level 3, **bosses call down lightning** too: a yellow mark shows where a bolt will strike, so move away or DODGE. They call more bolts at higher levels.
- **POWER** (or the G key) does your level's move, then needs about 6 seconds to recharge.
- **In BRAWL:** FLAME DASH charges into a fighter. From level 3, THUNDER strikes every fighter near you.

## Level (difficulty)

Tap **LEVEL** on the title screen to change it. The game remembers your choice.

| Level | Enemy health | Enemy hits | Enemy speed |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| EASY | a bit more than before | softer | slower |
| NORMAL | 2× (fights last about twice as long as before) | normal | normal |
| HARD | 2.8× | harder | faster |
| CHAMPION | 3.6× | much harder | much faster |

In BRAWL, higher levels make the CPU fighters attack more often and hit harder.

## The intro

When the game starts, a pencil draws Arthur, Nova, Rocko and a ninja on notebook paper, line by line, then colours them in and turns the page to the title. It takes about 4 seconds. Tap **SKIP**, or anywhere on the page, to skip it.

## BRAWL (like a platform fighter)

Tap **BRAWL**, pick your fighter, and fight three CPU fighters at once (ninjas can show up too), everyone against everyone. Fighters are smaller and the **camera follows the action**, zooming out when fighters are far apart and in when they're close.

- **Stages:** pick one with the **STAGE** button on the fighter-select screen, or leave it on RANDOM.
  - **Sky Ruins:** floating platforms
  - **Ramp Valley:** a hill with ramps up and down
  - **Pagoda Roofs:** sloped red roofs at night
  - **Pirate Ship:** masts and crow's nests. **Cannonballs** fly across the deck, so jump over them!
  - **Castle Walls:** towers, ramps and a high bridge
  - **Space Station:** **low gravity**, so you jump much higher and float down
  - **Snowy Peak:** **slippery ice**, so you slide when you stop
  - **Candy Land:** green **bounce pads** throw you into the air
  - **Robot Factory:** **conveyor belts** carry you along
  - **Tower Steps:** ramps, steps and high towers
  - **Jungle:** tree branches and a **vine swing** that sways from side to side. Watch out for falling **coconuts**!
  - **Dino Valley:** ride in the saddle of a big **T-Rex** up high, or a **Triceratops** down on the ground, as they walk back and forth under a volcano. **Raptors** sprint across the valley floor, so jump! A long-necked dinosaur walks in the distance and pterosaurs glide in the sky
  - **Highway:** a bridge with ramps and a **flatbed truck** you can ride. **Trucks** speed along the road, so jump!
  - **Fishing Pond:** two docks and a **raft** that floats between them. Fall in the water and you're out! **Fish** leap up out of the pond
- **Damage %:** the number at the top shows each fighter's damage. The higher it is, the further they fly when hit.
- **Lives:** the dots show lives (3 each). Get knocked off the screen and you lose one, then drop back in from the sky.
- **Win:** be the last fighter standing. The match ends if you lose all three lives.
- **Moves:**
  - **Walking and jumping:** walk, jump, and **double jump** in the air, with a front flip.
  - **Drop down:** push the stick **down** (or the down arrow) to drop through a thin platform.
  - **Attacks:** PUNCH is quick, KICK hits harder, DODGE makes you safe for a moment, and FIRE charges a fireball.
  - **SMASH:** when SUPER is full you glow with red fire. SUPER then does a SMASH that sends fighters flying.
- The yellow **P1** arrow shows which fighter is you.

## Practice

Tap **PRACTICE** on the title to try every move on a dummy that never hits back and never goes down. Leave with the ✕ button.

## Arcade

Fight wave after wave until you get knocked down.

- Waves 1 to 8 are Rival, Ivy, Brute, Spike Bot, the MEGA SPIKE boss, Ninja, the MASTER KAGE boss, and Quake. Every 9th wave is the Shadow King.
- Boss fights start with a **BOSS FIGHT!** warning and an alarm.
- Every wave, enemies get more health, hit a little harder, and attack faster.
- Clearing a wave gives you back 30 health.
- **Score** goes up for every hit, for combos, for perfect dodges and for every wave. **Best** is your top score. It shows on the title screen.

## Buttons

The buttons are along the bottom of the screen.

| Button | Keyboard | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ◀ LEFT / RIGHT ▶ | arrows | Step. **Hold** the button to keep walking. |
| JUMP | up arrow or space | Jump. **Tap again in the air** for a second, higher jump with a **front flip**. You can punch and kick in the air. |
| DODGE | C | Hop backwards and turn see-through. Nothing can hurt you while you dodge. |
| PUNCH | Z | Quick hit. With a boomerang, PUNCH throws it. **Hold** for a **METEOR PUNCH** (see below). |
| KICK | X | Steps in and hits harder. **Tap twice quickly** for a **WHIRLWIND KICK**, or in the air for a **HELICOPTER KICK**. **Hold** for a **SKY KICK**. |
| FIRE | F (hold) | **Hold** to charge a fireball, **let go** to shoot it. See below. |
| (BRAWL) drop down | down arrow | Push the joystick down to drop through a thin platform. |
| POWER | G | Your POWER move (from power level 2). It recharges in about 6 seconds. |
| SUPER | V | Your fighter's SUPER move. It works when the button is gold and you are on the ground. |
| ✕ (top middle) | | Go back to the title. |

You always turn to face the enemy, so you can fight from either side.

In the Android app these buttons are replaced by a thumb joystick and game-pad buttons, so you can walk and punch at the same time (see `ANDROID.md`).

## How the fighters move

The stick fighters move more like people now:

- **Joints:** arms and legs bend at the **elbows and knees**. The far arm and leg are a little darker, so the body looks like it has depth.
- **Moving:** they **breathe** while standing and take real **steps** when they walk, with a 4-step stride.
- **Attacks:** a punch **winds up** before it flies, and a kick **lifts the knee** first.
- **Getting hit:** a hit to the belly **folds** the body over; a hit to the head **snaps it back**.
- **Shadows:** everyone has a **shadow** on the floor that shrinks and fades when they jump, in BRAWL too.
- **Turning:** you face the way you walk, so you can turn and fight ninjas behind you.

## Cool moves

- **Fire aura.** When your SUPER is full, during a SUPER move, and when a fireball is fully charged, your fighter glows in **red fire**.

- **Combos.** Hit again within about a second to keep a combo going. Every 3rd hit in a row shows **COMBO x3!** (then x6, x9 ...) and does 3 extra damage.
- **SUPER meter.** The thin bar under your health fills when you land hits or get hurt. When it is full it flashes, the SUPER button turns gold, and you see **SUPER READY!** You can't be hurt during your SUPER.
- **Perfect dodge.** If you DODGE just as an attack would have hit you, you see **PERFECT DODGE!** You take no damage, a big chunk of SUPER fills up, and you get 100 points. Try it when Brute charges!
- **Tap ahead.** If you tap PUNCH while a kick is still going, the punch happens right after. Big fighting games call this an input buffer.

## Whirlwind Kick

Tap **KICK twice quickly**. Your fighter lifts off the ground and **spins** with one leg out, travelling forward and hitting up to **3 times**. Because it spins, it hits enemies on **both sides**, which is great against a ninja squad or an enemy wave. It needs about a second to recharge. It works in BRAWL too, knocking fighters away on both sides. (Careful: it's still a kick, so it hurts to spin into Spike Bot's spikes!)

## Helicopter Kick

Jump, then tap **KICK twice quickly** while you're in the air. Your fighter splits their legs out like the blades of a helicopter and **spins fast**, with whirling motion lines round them. The spinning holds them up, so they **float down slowly**, drifting forward and hitting everything on **both sides** about every sixth of a second (up to 7 hits). It shares the Whirlwind Kick's recharge.

## Power moves: hold PUNCH or KICK

A quick tap is still a normal punch or kick. **Hold** the button (or Z / X) and your fighter winds up and gathers power, trembling harder as it builds:

- **Hold PUNCH: METEOR PUNCH.** A **gold** ball of power grows in your fist. Let go and you **dash forward** and smash the enemy. The longer you held, the further the dash and the bigger the hit (about 3 punches' worth at full power).
- **Hold KICK: SKY KICK.** A **blue** ball of power grows at your foot. Let go and you fly **upwards** with a rising kick that hits **twice**.

After about a second you hear the *ding* and see **FULL POWER!**, your aura blazes, and the move also shakes the ground. Getting the timing right matters: while you charge, you can't dodge.

## Fireball

Hold **FIRE** and a glowing ball grows in your hand, with a rising sound. Let go to shoot it.

- **Quick tap:** a small, fast fireball (3 damage).
- **Hold about 1 second:** you hear a *ding* and see **FULL POWER!** Let go for a giant fireball that does 15 damage, explodes with a **BOOM**, and shakes the ground.
- Fireballs get past Spike Bot's spikes, and they can knock Ivy's and the Shadow King's shots out of the air (**BLOCKED!**). A full-power fireball keeps going after it blocks a shot.
- One fireball at a time: you can charge the next one when the last one is gone.

On the Android app, the FIRE button fills up gold as it charges.

## Weapons in hand

Swords, hammers, boomerangs and the bosses' weapons sit in the fighter's **front hand** and line up with the **forearm** in every pose: held up in the guard stance, thrust forward in a punch, hanging down while walking, and turning with the body in a flip. The build script works out the hand position for every picture from the jointed poses (the `Hand X / Hand Y / Hand Dir` lists for Hero, `EHand ...` for enemies).

## Easy remixes

All the fighter numbers are in the **Stage**, in the script `when I receive [hero picked]`. All the enemy numbers are in the Stage script `when I receive [load enemy]`. Change a number, click the green flag, and play.

- Make Arthur stronger: change his `set [Punch Power] to [5]` to `[7]`.
- Make the Shadow King easier: change his `set [Enemy Max] to [90]` to `[60]`.
- Make weapons fall more often: in the **Weapon Drop** sprite, change `wait (pick random [3] to [7])` to `[2] to [4]`.
- Make your own catchphrase: in the **Hero** sprite, `when I receive [fighters ready]`, change `say [I'm Arthur!]`.
- Draw a new fighter: costumes are named `arthur idle`, `arthur punch` and so on. Paint over them in the Costumes tab.

`projects/LEGENDS_BLOCKS.md` lists every script in every sprite, so you can find any block.

## Notes for Mitch

The `.sb3` and the block listing are built by a script, so they can be remade any time:

```bash
python3 tools/build_legends_sb3.py
```

The script checks the project before it writes it. It checks that every costume, backdrop, sound, variable and message exists, and that every message that is sent is also received.

`tools/test_legends.js` plays the built project in Scratch's own engine (scratch-vm), with no screen and a fake clock. A bot finishes Story mode with each of the three fighters and plays Arcade for six minutes. Then the test mashes every button at once and checks each weapon hits and breaks. It also checks that nothing gets stuck: Hero locked, the boomerang never returning, health out of range, the Quit, Retry and Home buttons.

```bash
npm install --prefix /tmp/scratch-tools scratch-vm
NODE_PATH=/tmp/scratch-tools/node_modules node tools/test_legends.js
```

How it is built:

- **Buttons never start long scripts.** In Scratch, a broadcast restarts a script that is already running. Two quick taps could leave a "busy" flag stuck on for the rest of the fight. So the buttons only set `Want Punch`, `Want Kick` and so on, and two loops in the Hero sprite act on them. LEFT and RIGHT still broadcast, because a step has no wait in it.
- **Hits use distance, not "touching".** Every hit checks how far apart the two fighters are. That works the same on every device, and it can be tested without a screen.
- **BRAWL's per-frame work is in custom blocks set to "run without screen refresh"** (`fighter frame`, `camera frame`, `fireball frame`). A normal Scratch `repeat` pauses for a frame on every turn, so looping over 4 fighters and 8 platforms would make everything move in slow motion.
- **Clones hear every message too.** Any sprite that makes clones checks `Is Clone` first, or each clone would make more clones; the build script refuses to build a sprite that forgets.
- **Hurt and stun use timestamps, not waits.** `Enemy Stun Until` and `Hero Safe Until` hold a `timer` value, so a hit that arrives mid-wait cannot break anything.
- Sounds are small beeps and thumps made by the build script, so there are no outside files.
- The main scripts have comments in the editor that explain what they do.
